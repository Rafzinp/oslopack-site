Refund policy

What happens if something arrives damaged, wrong or unused.

Shipping policyRefund policyPrivacy policyTerms of service
Last updated 1 October 2026

Damaged or wrong items

If cartons arrive damaged or you receive something you didn't order, tell us within 7 days of delivery and we will replace the items or refund them in full.

Step 1Report itEmail a photo of the carton and delivery slip.
Step 2We confirmWe reply within one working day.
Step 3Replace or refundReplacement ships in 48 hours, refunds in 5–7 days.

Unopened cartons

Unopened cartons of standard stock items can be returned within 14 days of delivery. We will collect them and refund the carton price. A 10% restocking fee applies when the return is not due to our error.

What we cannot refund

  • Custom printed cups, bags and boxes, which are made to order.
  • Opened sleeves or cartons, unless damaged on arrival.
  • Special-order items marked "non-returnable" on the quote.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Bank transfer and LPO invoices are credited to your account or refunded by transfer within 5–7 working days.
