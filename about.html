Started in a Doha café storeroom.

oslopack began when a café owner got tired of ordering cups by the sleeve from three different suppliers. We built one catalogue, bought by the carton, with one invoice and one delivery.

Today we hold stock in Doha, quote bulk orders the same day and print custom logos from five cartons. Everything we sell is traceable to a certified mill or factory.
