1Quotes and prices

Prices on the website are in Qatari riyals (QAR), per carton, and exclude any custom printing. Quotes issued through the Request for quote form are valid for 14 days. Prices may change when raw material costs change.

2Orders and payment

An order is confirmed when we send you an order confirmation. We accept bank transfer, card and company LPO. Approved business accounts may pay on net 30 terms, and invoices not paid by the due date can be charged a late fee.

3Delivery and risk

Delivery times are estimates. Risk of loss passes to you when the cartons are signed for at the delivery address. See the shipping policy for areas and times.

4Custom printed orders

  • Artwork must be approved by you in writing before production starts.
  • You confirm you have the right to use any logo or artwork you send.
  • Printed quantities may vary by up to 5% from the quantity ordered.

5Returns and liability

Returns and refunds follow our refund policy. Our liability for any order is limited to the price you paid for the affected items, to the extent allowed by Qatari law.

6Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Qatar. Any dispute is subject to the courts of Doha.
