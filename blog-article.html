Compostable vs recyclable cups: what your café should actually choose

Most "eco" cups end up in the same bin. Here is how to pick the right material for Qatar's waste system, and what it costs per carton.

LH
Layla Haddad · Packaging specialistNov 3, 20269 min read
On this pageThe short answerCompostable, recyclable, reusableWhat Qatar can processCost per cartonOur recommendation

If you run a café in Doha, you have probably been told to "go eco" by a supplier, a customer or both. The trouble is that three very different products all wear the same green label. Picking the wrong one can cost you more and still send the cup to landfill.

The short answer

For most cafés, a PE-free, home-compostable cup is the better choice when you also run a compost or collection scheme. Without one, a standard recyclable paper cup with a plant-based lining is cheaper and does the same job in practice.

  • Dine-in heavy café with food waste collection: choose compostable.
  • Mostly takeaway, mixed bins on the street: choose recyclable paper.
  • Office and event catering: ask for single-wall cups with fibre lids.

Compostable, recyclable, reusable

The three words are not interchangeable. A compostable cup breaks down in the right conditions, usually in an industrial facility. A recyclable cup can be turned back into paper, but only if the lining can be separated. A reusable cup avoids the question entirely, at the cost of washing and deposits.

The best cup is the one your customers' bin can actually handle.Layla Haddad, oslopack packaging specialist

What Qatar can process

Industrial composting capacity is growing but is still limited, so most compostable cups placed in general waste are landfilled with everything else. That does not make them a bad buy. It means the benefit depends on how your waste is collected.

Check before you switchAsk your waste contractor whether they accept certified compostable packaging. If not, a recyclable cup avoids paying a premium for a benefit you will not get.

Our recommendation

Start with what your bins and your contractor can handle, then choose the cup. If you serve 300 drinks a day, the compostable premium is roughly QAR 270 a month. That is small next to what customers think of your brand, but only worth paying once it does what the label promises.

A simple checklist

  • Confirm how your waste contractor treats compostable packaging.
  • Look for BPI or OK compost HOME certification on the carton.
  • Match the lid to the cup: fibre lids for compostable, PP lids for recyclable.
  • Order a sample carton before committing to a bulk quote.
